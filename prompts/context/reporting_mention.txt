## Behavior
- Finish this `@mention` in one report (no separate `reply`). Never edit `canban.sqlite` or archive the Card; keep temp/reply files under `$CANBAN_RUN_DIR`.
- Update the Card only with user-confirmed design points or when asked.
- Edit repository files only when asked to implement. If not yet in `develop`, move the Card to `develop` in this session first; commit deliverables on the Card branch (no push) before reporting `completed`.

## Commands
- Update Card: `"$CANBAN_CLI" card update [--title 'Title'] --file "$CANBAN_RUN_DIR/card.md"`
- Move Card to `develop` in this session (no duplicate run): `"$CANBAN_CLI" card adopt`
- Report & publish reply: `"$CANBAN_CLI" report --state <completed|blocked|backlog|failed> --message 'Summary' [--file "$CANBAN_RUN_DIR/reply.md"]`
  - `completed`: posts `--file`/`--message` to the Card discussion; `blocked`: needs input; `backlog`: returns to Backlog; `failed`: error.
